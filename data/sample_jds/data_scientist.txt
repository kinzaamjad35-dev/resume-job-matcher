Data Scientist (Mid-level) - Northwind Retail Analytics
SAMPLE DATA: fictional job description for demonstration.

About the role
We are looking for a Data Scientist to build demand forecasting and customer analytics models that guide pricing and inventory decisions across 300 stores.

Responsibilities
- Develop machine learning models for time-series forecasting and customer churn prediction
- Perform exploratory data analysis and feature engineering on large sales datasets
- Communicate findings to stakeholders through clear dashboards and presentations
- Deploy models into production with the engineering team and monitor their performance
- Design A/B tests and evaluate the impact of pricing experiments

Requirements
- 3+ years of experience in data science or machine learning
- Strong Python, pandas, scikit-learn and SQL skills
- Experience with gradient boosting (XGBoost or LightGBM)
- Solid statistics: hypothesis testing and experiment design
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Statistics, Mathematics or a related field

Nice to have
- Experience with AWS or GCP, Docker and MLOps tooling such as MLflow
- Data visualization with Plotly, Tableau or Power BI
- Familiarity with Spark
