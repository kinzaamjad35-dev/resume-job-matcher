About the role
Lumen is an online homeware brand. We need a Digital Marketing Manager to grow traffic, conversions and brand awareness.

Responsibilities
- Plan and run paid advertising campaigns on Google Ads and Meta Ads
- Lead SEO and content marketing strategy
- Manage social media channels and email marketing campaigns
- Report on performance using Google Analytics and manage a monthly budget

Requirements
- 4+ years of experience in digital marketing
- Proven results with SEO, paid advertising and email marketing
- Strong Google Analytics and Excel skills
- Excellent communication and leadership
- Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Business or a related field

Nice to have
- HubSpot or Salesforce CRM experience
- Basic SQL or Tableau for reporting
